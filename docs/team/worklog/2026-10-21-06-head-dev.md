### 2026-10-21 | head-dev | 1h 30m

- The sea-time log can now record time. The big button on the log's home screen checks you in and out, with a running timer. It remembers your last boat, job and waters, so checking in again is one tap. Only one trip can be open at a time on a phone.
- Past days can be added afterwards ("backfilled"), but only after the user agrees on a screen that says these days are self-reported. Every backfilled day is labeled that way and records when the user agreed.
- Each entry records the boat, the job aboard and the waters (seven kinds, from the Coast Guard rules). A GPS suggestion has its own slot and is never treated as the user's answer.
- Any entry under 4 hours is marked "Not eligible: under 4 hours" and stays in the list. Entries of 4 to 8 hours and of 12 hours or more show a short note on how the Coast Guard may count them. The app counts no days; that comes later.
- Phones that already had the log keep everything they saved.
- Files: src/log/entry.ts, src/log/schema.ts, src/log/store.ts, src/log/entry-screens.ts, src/log/screens.ts, src/log/main.ts, log/index.html, tests and e2e for entries
