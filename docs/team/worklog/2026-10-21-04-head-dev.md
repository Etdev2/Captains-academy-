### 2026-10-21 | head-dev | 1h 30m

- The sea-time log now keeps a list of the boats you work on. Only a name is needed at first. The rest (official number, tonnage, length, propulsion, owner, horsepower and a photo) can be filled in later, and the list shows what is still missing.
- Tonnage is the official figure from the boat's papers and nothing else. There is no way to estimate it yet, because nobody has confirmed the Coast Guard accepts an estimate.
- The log also has a fixed list of deck and engine jobs, and each boat can have a usual job. The list says nothing about how a job counts toward a license, because that is still undecided.
- Boats and jobs are saved on the phone, survive a reload with no signal, and delete with two taps so a stray thumb can't remove one. Phones that already had the log keep their saved entries when it upgrades.
- The reviewer found that a photo still being prepared could be saved onto the wrong boat if you moved to another one quickly. Saving now waits for the photo, and a photo meant for another boat is dropped. A quick double tap on Save no longer adds the same boat twice.
- Files: src/log/entry.ts, src/log/schema.ts, src/log/store.ts, src/log/screens.ts, src/log/main.ts, log/index.html, tests and e2e for vessels
