Rules of the Road · Lights and Shapes · Rules 20–31

Night Harbor Run

One lap, 15 gates, one encounter at each. Every other vessel shows only her navigation lights, and you only see the lights whose arcs face you. Call what she is, then say what you do. One leg runs by day with shapes, and one runs in fog with nothing but sound.

White: masthead 225°, stern 135°, all-round 360° Red port 112.5° Green starboard 112.5° Yellow towing light
← → or A D: steer
↑ ↓ or W S: throttle
Space: call it early (bonus)
H: auto-helm toward the next gate
Sound on: horns and spoken answers
Training prototype. The rule wording here is a draft and has not been checked against the official Navigation Rules text. Distances, light ranges (Rule 22) and fog-signal intervals are compressed so the lap fits in a few minutes. Vertical spacing of lights is exaggerated so it can be read on a phone.
